**Title:** Lili Marleen – Damascus
**Author:** Roy Prinzessin (RATIONALTHEATER MÜNCHEN e.V.)
**Target:** ~1 hour animated film.
**Visual style:** *Waltz with Bashir* — oscillating between rotoscoped-real
footage and hand-drawn animation; muted, dusty palette; high-contrast ink;
war-memory dream tone.
**Production cadence (director's note):** week-by-week, ~1 year project.

---

## Logline

A disciplined major saves the woman he loves by breaking her heart and
exiling her from the war — then destroys himself with the order he gives
three weeks later. Years on she fights her way back to him, only for the
images of dead children to follow them both to the end.

## Principal characters

- **Major P** — career officer in Kunduz, Afghanistan (age 35 in 2010).
  Disciplined, guarded, drawn to LM's idealism which he mistakes for
  naïveté. Authorizes the strike that ruins him; spirals into addiction and
  guilt. See `seed/character_bible.md`.
- **Lieutenant LM ("Lili Marleen")** — aristocratic, rebellious idealist who
  came to build schools for girls. Later Captain, decorated, who resigns,
  unravels, and dies by suicide. See `seed/character_bible.md`.

## Chapter → scene map (9 chapters; Writer should expand to scenes/shots)

- **I. Kunduz, first meeting (2010).** Camp courtyard; rooftop nights;
  building schools; "You are my moral axis."
- **II. The kiss (summer 2010) & the threat.** New school inaugurated; the
  letter — a crudely drawn crucified girl — found by the newspaper/mattress.
- **III. The transfer.** P forges the strategic reason; LM breaks; the crack
  in the door he later touches.
- **IV. Clearance granted.** The truck strike; the five boys under the
  vehicle siphoning gas; the smoke and screaming; P's collapse into drink
  and drugs.
- **V. (2025) Reunion at the Syrian border.** LM transfers in to find him;
  the ruined man; "You look like a person who lost their soul somewhere."
- **VI. Resignation.** Captain LM declines the medal, resigns; the wars blur;
  cutting; three suicide attempts.
- **VII. Petty crime as a plea for punishment.** Perfume; break-ins; the toy
  gun; declared mentally incompetent → psychiatric ward.
- **VIII. The ward.** The psychiatrist confidant; contact forbidden; the
  final suicide attempt.
- **IX. Coda.** Kabul's last soldier; Berlin's parliament; Munich — an old
  man opens a letter that smells of jasmine and weeps. "The wind in
  Afghanistan carried more than just sand. It carried the ashes of
  everything that could have been."

## Key environments (Environment bible seeds — "places")

- **Afghan Camp (Kunduz, 2010)** — courtyard, tents, watchtower, generators,
  rooftop where they talk at night. Dusty, lamplit, fortified.
- **Munich Apartment (present)** — quiet, aged, interior; the jasmine letter.
- **Syrian Border Center (2025)** — air-traffic-control room, screens.
- **Psychiatric Ward** — clinical, cold, institutional.
- (also: the village/new school, the desert road of the truck strike.)

## Key props

- The threatening **letter** (crucified-girl symbol).
- The **jasmine-scented letter** in the coda.
- Dog tags; field chair; the cracked door.

## Tone & continuity constraints (for the Writer/Critic)

- Two timelines: **2010 Afghanistan** (younger, sharp P) and **2025 Syria /
  present Germany** (ruined, gray, bloated P). Keep the two looks of P
  consistent and distinct.
- Restraint over melodrama; silence as a beat. War-memory haze.
- Sensitive subject matter — depict consequences with gravity, not
  spectacle.
